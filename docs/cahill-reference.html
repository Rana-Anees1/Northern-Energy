Our Process Made Simple

We like everything to be simple and easy, from beginning to end.

1

Telephone Consultation

We use satellite imagery to measure your roof and remotely generate your solar PV system installation proposal. This can be refined until you’re happy with the design, brand choices and solar installation cost, ensuring it aligns perfectly with your goals and budget.
You can get started here

2

In Person On Site Survey

We don’t believe in pushy sales visits—just honest advice. That’s why we’ll send an independent surveyor to assess cable runs and check the best locations for your equipment installation. once you're happy for a survey.

We’ll ensure the remote design is accurate and nothing is missed so that the photovoltaic system installation can proceed.

3

Solar System Installation Day

After receiving grid approval for your solar installation and arranging scaffolding, our in-house roofers and electricians complete your solar installation typically in one day.

Once complete we guide you through the monitoring platform and help you get the most out of your system.

Home Solar And Batteries: a local solution for global problems.

There’s more solar being installed now than ever and is it any wonder why? Recent energy price hikes have increased their cost-effectiveness and made solar panels an even more attractive option for most homeowners to consider.

Solar and battery installations are a low-maintenance ‘set and forget’ technology: a power plant of your own that pays for itself, increases your property value and lowers your carbon footprint.

With our cars, heating and the world around us becoming more dependent on electricity, it’s only a matter of time before there’s solar everywhere.
